DESIGN KIT · v1.0
ARCLIGHT
This is the visual system for the Arclight media server. It has three layers: a dark glass interface, a perspective grid floor with a single light source on the horizon, and thin circuit traces with 45° bends. Cyan is the only accent. Orange and red mean something is wrong. Screens are in Arclight Pages, and copy-paste tokens are in handoff/arclight.css.

Typography
There are two families. Archivo (variable width axis) is used for all UI text and headings. Headings are set wide (118–125%) and body text at normal width. JetBrains Mono is for numbers, paths, codes, format strings and section labels only, never for sentences. Text uses Title case only on page titles; everything else uses Sentence case, and ALL CAPS is reserved for the spaced section label.

06
Light, glow & circuitry
GLOW = LIVE
Only three things glow: the connection dot, the active navigation bar, and active progress. Everything else is a flat line. If two things on screen glow and neither is live, remove the glow from both.
CIRCUIT TRACE
Traces are 1px lines at 50% cyan. They run only horizontally, vertically, or at 45°, and each end finishes in a hollow 8px node. A trace must connect two real elements. Use at most two per screen. Background circuit bundles are 16% opacity and kept behind the glass.
BACKDROP
Every page has a perspective grid floor, a horizon at about 52% of the height, and a single spire of light at the vanishing point. The spire is background only; never place UI on it or point UI at it. Keep the middle of the screen open where possible so it shows. A 1px scanline at 1.5% white and a vignette sit on top of the floor.
