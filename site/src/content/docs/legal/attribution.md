## TMDB

This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

Where Arclight shows TMDB data, the TMDB logo is shown as TMDB requires. Movie and TV metadata and images come from [The Movie Database](https://www.themoviedb.org/).

## Other data sources

Depending on how a server operator configures it, Arclight can fetch data from the services below. Each service's data comes under its own license or terms.

| Source | Used for | License or terms |
|---|---|---|
| [MusicBrainz](https://musicbrainz.org/) | Music metadata | Core data is CC0 (public domain). Some supplementary data is CC BY-NC-SA. |
| [Cover Art Archive](https://coverartarchive.org/) | Album artwork | Images are under various terms set by their uploaders. |
| [Open Library](https://openlibrary.org/) | Book metadata | Data is available under CC0. |
| [Wikidata](https://www.wikidata.org/) | Linked identifiers and facts | CC0 |
| [Wikipedia](https://www.wikipedia.org/) | Summaries and text | CC BY-SA |
| [Fanart.tv](https://fanart.tv/) | Artwork | Subject to Fanart.tv's terms |
| [ListenBrainz](https://listenbrainz.org/) | Music listening data | Subject to its terms of use |
| [Audnexus](https://audnex.us/) | Audiobook metadata | Subject to its terms |

Data from these sources stays under the licenses above. Arclight does not claim ownership of it. Please check each service for its current terms.
