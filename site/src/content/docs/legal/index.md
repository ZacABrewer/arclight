> Short version: Arclight is free, open-source software that you run yourself. The Arclight project does not collect data from your server, does not track you and does not sell anything. These pages explain that in detail.

## Pages

**[Privacy policy](/legal/privacy/)**: what the website, the mobile apps and the push relay do (and do not do) with data, and your rights under GDPR and CCPA/CPRA.

**[App privacy details](/legal/app-privacy/)**: a plain table that mirrors the Apple App Store privacy label and the Google Play Data safety form.

**[Terms of use](/legal/terms/)**: the rules for using the website and the mobile apps. The software itself is governed by the GPL-3.0.

**[Delete your data](/legal/data-deletion/)**: how to remove the data held on your device, on a server and at the push relay.

**[Open-source licenses](/legal/licenses/)**: the GPL-3.0, third-party components, fonts and how to use the Arclight name and logo.

**[Copyright and content policy](/legal/copyright/)**: what the project hosts (no media), and how to send a copyright notice.

**[Attribution and non-affiliation](/legal/attribution/)**: the TMDB notice, data-source credits and who Arclight is not connected to.

**[Security policy](/legal/security/)**: supported versions, how to report a vulnerability, and how the apps and relay protect data.
